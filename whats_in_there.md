# What is agent.py?

Imagine `agent.py` is the **Team Captain** or the **Boss**. 

The captain doesn't do all the hard work alone. Instead, the captain organizes a team of helpers to get a big job done. 

Here is what the captain (`agent.py`) tells the helpers to do:

1. 🕵️‍♂️ **The Scout (`MovieService`):** The captain says, *"Go find out what new movies are coming out in theaters this week!"*
2. 👂 **The Listener (`SearchService`):** The captain says, *"Go on the internet and listen to what people are whispering about these new movies. Are they excited?"*
3. 🧠 **The Smart Judge (`RankingService`):** The captain says, *"Look at the movies and what people are saying, and use your big brain to decide if they are going to be good or bad."*
4. 📬 **The Mail Carrier (`EmailService`):** Finally, the captain says, *"Take all these reviews, put them in a nice letter, and email it to my friend so they know what to watch!"*

So, the `agent.py` file is just the instruction manual that tells all the different parts of the program how to work together in the right order to send you your weekly movie email!
